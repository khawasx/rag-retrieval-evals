# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Covered expenses

Travel, client meals, and approved software subscriptions are reimbursable
when pre-approved by a manager. Receipts are required for amounts over $25.

## Submission deadline

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the cost.
Late submissions may be denied except for documented emergencies.

## Per diem rates

Domestic travel per diem is $65 per day. International per diem follows
the finance department rate table published each quarter.

## Prohibited expenses

Personal entertainment, alcohol without client presence, and fines or
penalties are not reimbursable under any circumstances.
