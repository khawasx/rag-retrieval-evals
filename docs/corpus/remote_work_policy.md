# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees may request remote work after completing probation.
Managers approve schedules based on team coverage requirements.

## Core hours

Employees must be available 10:00–16:00 in their local timezone for meetings
and incident response. Outside core hours, async communication is acceptable.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and monitor stipend of $500 annually.
Employees are responsible for securing home network access with WPA2 or better.

## Security

Remote workers must use the company VPN for accessing internal systems.
Storing customer data on personal devices is prohibited.
