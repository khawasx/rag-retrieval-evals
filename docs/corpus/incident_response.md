# Incident Response Playbook

## Severity levels

P1 incidents involve production outages or active security breaches.
P2 incidents cover major feature degradation affecting paid customers.
P3 incidents are minor bugs with acceptable workarounds.

## P1 response time

On-call engineers must acknowledge P1 alerts within 15 minutes.
A status page update is required within 30 minutes of confirmation.

## Escalation

If unresolved after 60 minutes, incident commanders escalate to engineering
leadership and customer success for enterprise accounts.

## Post-incident review

All P1 and P2 incidents require a blameless postmortem within 5 business
days, including timeline, root cause, and corrective actions.
